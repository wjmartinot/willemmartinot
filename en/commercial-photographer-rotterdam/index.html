Frequently asked questions

Do you work as a corporate photographer across Rotterdam and surroundings? + −

Yes. From The Hague I can reach all of Rotterdam within half an hour — including the centre, Kop van Zuid, the WTC, Kralingen, Schiedam and the Rotterdam port area.

Do you charge travel costs for corporate photography in Rotterdam? + −

For assignments in Rotterdam I do not charge extra travel time; only any on-site parking costs are stated clearly in the quote in advance.

How quickly do we receive photos after an event or shoot in Rotterdam? + −

For conferences and business gatherings, an initial selection for press releases and LinkedIn is typically ready the same day or the next business day. Complete reportages and portraits follow within 3 to 5 business days.

Can we combine a corporate reportage with business portraits? + −

Yes, that happens often. During a half or full day we combine workplace atmosphere shots with new LinkedIn headshots or on-location team portraits.

Do you bring studio lighting to our Rotterdam location? + −

Yes. For portraits at the office and gatherings with little daylight I bring a compact mobile lighting kit for a calm, consistent look.

How are the photos delivered, and what about usage rights? + −

You receive the retouched selection digitally via an online gallery in high resolution (for print) and web format, including unlimited business usage rights.

What does a corporate photographer in Rotterdam cost? + −

For on-location corporate assignments in Rotterdam, transparent guide prices apply: from €650 excl. VAT for a half day (up to 4 hours) and €950 to €1,350 excl. VAT for a full day (up to 8 hours), including preparation, retouching and business usage rights.
