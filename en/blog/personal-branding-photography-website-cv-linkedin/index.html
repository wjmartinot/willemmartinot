Personal branding photography: more than a LinkedIn photo

A health coach, a psychiatrist, a VU lecturer, a real-estate advisor, a lawyer and a social worker. They all came to my studio in The Hague recently. Not only for a new LinkedIn photo, but for images for their own website and CV.

That is personal branding: letting your photos show who you are and what you stand for, everywhere someone meets you for the first time.

Six professions, six stories

What they share: they work under their own name, or their name is their calling card. A potential client, student or client first sees their photo — and only then reads what they do.

Still, everyone wanted to project something different.

Same photographer, often the same studio. But every photo tells a different story.

Why one photo is not enough

A LinkedIn photo is a square of a few centimetres. On your website you need more: a wide image at the top of your homepage, a portrait next to your about text, perhaps an image for your contact page. And on your CV you want a calm, professional portrait that still works small.

That is why a personal branding shoot delivers a series rather than a single photo:

  • Landscape frames with room for text, for your website header
  • Standing portraits for your about page and social media
  • A clean headshot for LinkedIn and your CV
  • Variation in expression: serious, lightly smiling and genuinely laughing

So you have one recognisable style everywhere people meet you.

How a personal branding shoot works

  1. The conversation. We start with who you want to reach and what they should feel when they see your photo. Trust, expertise, energy, calm. That shapes everything that follows.
  2. Clothing and atmosphere. Based on that conversation we choose what you wear and which background fits: suit or blazer, studio or outdoor location, light or dark.
  3. The shoot. In the The Hague studio or on location. I guide posture and expression so you do not have to pose — you can simply be who you are.
  4. The selection. You receive an edited set in the formats you need for your website, CV and LinkedIn.

Your photo works before you say a word

Whether you run a practice, teach or advise: people choose a person. A photo that matches who you are makes that choice easier.
