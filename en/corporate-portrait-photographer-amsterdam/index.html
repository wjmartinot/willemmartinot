Difference between a corporate portrait and conference photography

A corporate portrait is controlled: light, pose and presence serve the brand or person. Conference photography is documentary — speakers, audience and networking moments in the room, often without directing. Both belong to professional visibility, but they ask for a different approach; many organisations combine them in one communications strategy.

My cinematic style — light, atmosphere and rhythm — is not a gimmick, but useful for campaigns, employer branding and annual reports. Clients recognise that quality in portraits and in atmospheric images alongside formal reportage.

Frequently asked questions

What does a portrait photoshoot in Amsterdam cost? + −

An individual portrait shoot starts from €250. The exact price depends on duration, location, the number of people and how the images will be used. For teams and organisations I prepare a custom quote. See the rates for an indication or request a no-obligation quote.

How can I book a portrait shoot? + −

Get in touch via the contact form or by email. We briefly discuss your wishes, preferred location and planning. You then receive a confirmation with date, time and practical information.

How soon can a shoot take place? + −

In many cases a portrait shoot can be scheduled within a few days to two weeks, depending on availability. For urgent requests I look at short-term options — feel free to get in touch.

I do not like being in front of the camera — is that a problem? + −

No — that is exactly when it helps. Most people are not models and feel awkward in front of the lens. During the shoot I guide you calmly with clear directions on pose and gaze. The goal is a natural portrait that looks professional, without a stiff or forced look.

How long does a portrait shoot take? + −

An individual shoot usually takes 30 to 60 minutes. For teams or multiple employees I allow 15 to 20 minutes per person, depending on the number of images and locations. We schedule enough time so nobody feels rushed.

What should I wear during the shoot? + −

Choose clothing that fits your professional presence: calm colours, simple patterns and a well-fitting outfit. Avoid large logos or distracting prints. Bring a second option if you like. I am happy to advise beforehand on what works best for your sector and goal.

Where does the portrait shoot take place in Amsterdam? + −

On location (for example your office in Amsterdam, outdoors in the city) or in a studio setting in the region. For teams I regularly come on site with a mobile studio setup. Together we choose what best fits the desired look.

What is better: studio or location? + −

A studio gives a calm, consistent look with control over light and background — ideal for LinkedIn and team portraits. A location adds context and personality and can strengthen your organisation's story. Both options deliver professional results; the choice depends on the goal.

What can I use the portrait photos for? + −

Portraits are used for LinkedIn, websites, presentations, press materials, annual reports and internal communications. Images are delivered in high resolution for print and optimised versions for web and social media.

When will I receive the photos? + −

After the shoot I make a careful selection and professionally retouch the final images. You typically receive the photos within two to five working days via a secure download link. For larger series or teams we agree the delivery time in advance.

May I use the photos freely? + −

Yes, for the agreed use (website, LinkedIn, publications, internal communications). Usage rights are set out in the quote in advance. Extra use — for example advertising or large-run print — can be extended by agreement.

Can you also photograph multiple employees? + −

Yes, I regularly run portrait shoots for teams and organisations in Amsterdam and the surrounding area. That can be at the office with a mobile studio or at a central location. I plan the day efficiently so each employee gets enough time without disrupting work.

Do you ensure a consistent look within a team? + −

Yes. With consistent lighting, background and retouching, all portraits get a uniform, professional look. That matters for websites, LinkedIn pages and annual reports where employees appear side by side.

What is the difference between a corporate portrait and a LinkedIn headshot? + −

A corporate portrait is broader in use (website, press, presentations). A LinkedIn headshot is specifically optimised for the LinkedIn format: a sharp presence, neutral background and a composition that works well in a small profile circle. I offer both; often we make images in one session that suit both purposes.

Can a portrait shoot take place at my office in Amsterdam? + −

Yes — that is common. I come on location with compact equipment and, if needed, set up a mobile studio in a meeting room or office. Staff do not need to travel and the shoot fits into the working day. Also see my work as a photographer in Amsterdam and business photographer in Amsterdam.

Which file formats do you deliver? + −

You receive the images digitally as JPEG (high quality, suitable for web and print) via a download link. On request I also deliver TIFF or other formats. All images are professionally retouched and ready for immediate use.
