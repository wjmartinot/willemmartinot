Frequently asked questions

What does a business photographer do? + −

A business photographer creates images that represent an organisation visually: on-location reports, team photos, workplace situations, products and campaign imagery. The goal is strong, credible photography for websites, annual reports, press, social media and internal communications.

Where do you shoot corporate reports? + −

On location at the client: office, laboratory, production environment, construction site or event. I work with mobile lighting and compact setups so production runs efficiently without disrupting day-to-day operations. Rotterdam, The Hague and the wider Randstad.

What are commercial photos used for? + −

For websites, employer branding, recruitment, annual reports, press releases, social media, presentations and internal communications. Strong commercial photography reinforces the brand and shows what an organisation stands for.

How does a commercial photography assignment work? + −

We start with a briefing on goals, location and desired images. I plan around your working day. During the shoot I work independently and unobtrusively. Afterwards I deliver a selection of professionally edited images digitally.

Can you also build a complete image library? + −

Yes. For organisations that need strong imagery on an ongoing basis, I help build an image bank: a set of portraits, atmosphere shots and workplace situations that remains usable for years across channels.

How quickly are the images available? + −

A first selection can often be delivered within a few days. The full edited set follows within the agreed timeframe. For urgent communications, rush delivery is possible by arrangement.

Do you also work outside Rotterdam? + −

Yes, I regularly photograph in Amsterdam, The Hague and elsewhere in the Netherlands. Rotterdam is my working area; my studio is in The Hague.

What does commercial photography cost in Rotterdam? + −

Corporate reports and commercial photography start from €500, depending on scope, location and usage. Smaller assignments or combinations with portrait photography may cost less. See rates for an indication or request a custom quote.
