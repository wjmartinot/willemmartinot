Frequently asked questions

Where is your photo studio based, and do you also work on location? + −

My photo studio is based in The Hague. I also work on location at companies, government offices and event venues with a mobile lighting kit and compact gear.

Do you work as a corporate photographer outside The Hague as well? + −

Yes. From The Hague I work weekly for clients in Rotterdam, Amsterdam, Delft, Leiden and across the Netherlands.

How quickly do we receive the photos after an event or shoot? + −

For conferences and corporate events, an initial selection for press releases and LinkedIn is usually available the same day or the next business day. Full corporate reportages and portraits are delivered within 3 to 5 business days.

Can we combine event photography, corporate reportage and business portraits? + −

Yes, that happens regularly. In a half-day or full-day session we can capture atmosphere from a meeting or workplace as well as individual headshots of speakers or staff.

Do you also offer LinkedIn headshots for individuals and teams? + −

Yes — for individual professionals in the The Hague studio as well as complete teams at the office, with calm guidance for a natural, professional look.

How are the photos delivered, and what about usage rights? + −

You receive the retouched selection digitally via a clear online gallery in high resolution (for print) and optimised web format, including unlimited business usage rights for website, annual report, press and social media.

What experience do you have with government, embassies and international organisations? + −

With more than 25 years of experience I regularly photograph conferences, diplomatic receptions and corporate reportages for clients including the World Forum, TU Delft, embassies and international organisations across the Randstad.

What does a corporate photographer in The Hague cost? + −

For on-location corporate assignments, clear guide prices apply: from €650 excl. VAT for a half day (up to 4 hours) and €950 to €1,350 excl. VAT for a full day (up to 8 hours), including preparation, retouching and business usage rights. Separate packages are available for individual LinkedIn portraits in the studio.
