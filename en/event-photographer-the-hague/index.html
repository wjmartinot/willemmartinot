Frequently asked questions

Why do organisations book you as an event photographer? + −

I have photographed corporate events and conferences in The Hague and the Randstad for more than 25 years. Clients choose me for discreet presence, fast press selection and images ready for LinkedIn, press releases and internal communications.

Do you photograph at venues such as World Forum, Fokker Terminal, embassies and Sociëteit De Witte? + −

Yes. I regularly photograph conferences and corporate events at World Forum, Fokker Terminal, embassies, Sociëteit De Witte and TU Delft. I know the logistics of these venues and work discreetly within tight programmes.

How quickly do we receive photos after an event? + −

An initial selection for press releases, LinkedIn and internal updates is typically available the same day or the next business day. The full retouched set follows within 3 to 5 business days, in high resolution (print) and web format.

How many photographers do you recommend for a conference? + −

For single-track one-day conferences, one photographer is usually enough. For multiple rooms, parallel sessions or a dense networking programme I recommend a second photographer so key moments are not missed.

Do you work discreetly during the programme? + −

Yes. I move quietly through the room, use silent shutters where possible and never stand between speaker and audience. The event should run undisturbed; you notice my presence mainly afterwards in the quality of the images.

Can we also arrange speaker or VIP portraits during the event? + −

Yes. Many clients combine conference coverage with short portrait sessions in between or afterwards — useful for press kits, websites and LinkedIn without a separate shoot day.

What does an event photographer in The Hague cost? + −

Clear guide prices apply for corporate event photography: from €650 excl. VAT for a half day (up to 4 hours) and €950 to €1,350 excl. VAT for a full day (up to 8 hours), including preparation, retouching and business usage rights. Multi-day conferences or rush delivery are quoted separately.
